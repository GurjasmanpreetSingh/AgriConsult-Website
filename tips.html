Tips & Tricks for Farmers

Practical, low-cost techniques you can apply this season — tested on real farms across India.

Companion Planting

Plant marigolds near tomatoes to deter nematodes. Basil repels flies and improves tomato flavour. Beans fix nitrogen for corn.

Straw Mulching

Spread 2–3 inches of straw around plants. Retains moisture, blocks weeds, and decomposes into organic matter.

Early Morning Watering

Water before 8 AM. Reduces evaporation by 30% and prevents fungal diseases. Drip irrigation is even better.

DIY pH Test

Mix soil with vinegar — if it fizzes, soil is alkaline. Mix with baking soda + water — if fizzes, acidic. Neither = neutral.

Windbreaks

Plant tall grasses like vetiver on field edges. Cuts wind damage by up to 50% and prevents topsoil erosion.

Crop Rotation

Never grow the same crop family in the same spot 2 years in a row. Breaks pest and disease cycles naturally.

Attract Beneficial Insects

Plant dill, fennel, and sunflowers around your farm. Ladybugs and lacewings will naturally control aphids.

Olla Irrigation

Bury porous clay pots near plants. They seep water slowly into root zone — saves up to 70% water vs flood irrigation.

Solar-Powered Fencing

Low-cost solar fence kits keep wild animals out. Ideal for remote fields with no electric supply.

Weekly Farmer Tip

Test your soil pH every 6 months. Correcting pH can increase nutrient uptake by 30% — often more impactful than adding fertilizer.
